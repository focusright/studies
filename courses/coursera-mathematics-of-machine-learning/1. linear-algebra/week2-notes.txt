PRACTICE QUIZ
Changing Basis

v = [5 -1], b1 = [1 1], b2 = [1 -1], find v in terms of b1 + b2.
The scalar product v.b1/|b1|² is the projection of v onto b1, call this s1
The scalar product v.b2/|b2|² is the projection of v onto b2, call this s2
          v.b1          5*1 + -1*1            5-1
s1 * b1 = ----- * b1 =  ---------- * [1 1] =  --- [1 1] = 2 * [1 1] = [2 2]
          |b1|²          1² + 1²               2

          v.b2         5*1 + -1*-1           5+1
s2 * b2 = ----- * b2 = ----------- [1 -1] = ----- [1 -1] = 3 * [1 -1] = [3 -3]
          |b2|²         1² + -1²              2

adding the two above results gets v in the original basis
[2 2] + [3 -3] = [5 -1] = v
In the new basis b, v = [s1 s2] = [2 3]

For the rest see change-basis.py
