Take just |c d||0| for example, it ends up being |c|, that means a basis unit vector x
has been lifted out of its single dimension and taken into the 2nd dimension with
the new y value scaled by c. At the same time the x value also was scaled by a.

         |a b||0|               |b|
Now take |c d||1|, this becomes |d|, so the basis unit vector y also gets upgraded
with a 2nd dimension and scaled in the 1st, these two new vectors when added together
yields the resulting vector |a + b|
                            |c + d|
